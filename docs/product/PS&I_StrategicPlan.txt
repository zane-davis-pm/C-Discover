## Brief Summary

The document outlines the 2025 PS&I strategic plan realignment across seven focus areas:

- **Marketing, External Networking, and Client Targeting:** Strengthen lead generation through refreshed target lists, thought leadership, networking, VLP connections, and aligned conference/event strategy.
- **Client Entrenchment:** Build longer-term, multi-service client relationships through formal account management, client satisfaction processes, project closeout touchpoints, and expanded service introductions.
- **Cross-Selling:** Increase referrals and shared opportunities across firm practices by educating internal teams, building playbooks/case studies, and developing bundled service offerings.
- **New/Expanded Services:** Identify and pilot new service lines through competitor analysis, market research, client workshops, internal capability reviews, and cost/benefit assessment.
- **Staffing, Training, and Onboarding:** Improve staff readiness and capacity through better resource planning, onboarding, training, apprenticeship models, recruiting, and internal skill visibility.
- **Quality and Standardization:** Strengthen quality through standardized project management, QA processes, deliverable review plans, client feedback, and key project templates/checklists.
- **Internal Improvements/Governance:** Create clearer governance and continuous improvement processes with defined decision-making, prioritization, accountability, communication, and transparency.
